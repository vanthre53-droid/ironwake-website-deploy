# 08 — SOCIAL MEDIA AS A TRUST AND ACQUISITION WORKSTREAM
Social is not a footer link. It is proof, acquisition, and CRM attribution.

## Sequence (one platform at a time)
Eligibility check (incl. platform minimum-age and business-account rules) → ownership and
security setup by the human → profile copy/assets drafted by AI, approved by human →
publication by human → logged-out verification of every CTA and contact route → URL added
to the verified registry → only then may the website link to it.

## Absolute rules
- Never invent followers, engagement, testimonials, partnerships, badges, or verification.
- Never automate unsolicited DMs, follows, comments, or engagement.
- Never request or handle passwords, 2FA codes, or recovery material.
- A platform that is not verified live and logged-out does not appear on the website.
- Minimise storage of private message content; store attribution, not conversations.
