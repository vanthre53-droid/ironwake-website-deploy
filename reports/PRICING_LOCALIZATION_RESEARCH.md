## Recommended launch presentation

Use an outcome-led five-offer ladder internally: Business Leak Audit; Lead Capture/Trust Foundation; Missed-Lead Recovery; Booking Control; AI Reception Pilot. Show only approved offers publicly. Until the owner supplies capacity/cost data and approves a price ledger, public pages should use `Request scope` and state that provider usage, hosting, domain, payment fees, and taxes are separate where applicable.

## Market policy

1. Start every recommendation with the smallest sufficient scope.
2. If India and global work differ materially in currency, support window, localization, provider costs, tax/invoicing, compliance, or delivery scope, use explicit `/in/` and `/global/` pricing routes or a persistent explicit selector—not IP redirection.
3. If scope is the same, use one base structure with transparent currency/tax notes rather than hidden geographic price discrimination.
4. Never use a competitor's plan price as IronWake's cost floor. The reviewed providers use fundamentally different bundles, units, regions, and human-service assumptions.
5. Payments stay off at launch unless G1/G4 approve a fixed offer, adult/legal owner, KYC, refund/cancellation terms, taxes, provider, and a reconciliation model.

## Required price-ledger fields before publication

`offer_id`, market, approved scope version, currency, setup price/range, monthly price/range, included deliverables/hours, excluded usage costs, capacity assumption, provider-cost source/date, taxes/invoice treatment, cancellation/refund owner, approver/date, review date.

## Current research observations

Public category packages range from self-serve product plans to managed, multi-channel service plans. [RingCentral AI Receptionist](https://www.ringcentral.com/pricing/ai-receptionist.html) currently displays a USD 49/month standalone starting point with 100 minutes, while [Smith.ai](https://smith.ai/pricing/ai-receptionist) currently routes visitors through a needs and call-volume form. [SHIM AI](https://www.shimai.in/) markets India-based voice reception and appointment handling, but its activity and technology claims remain vendor assertions. These show that buyers expect scope and usage boundaries; they do not establish IronWake's costs, capacity, compliance duties, or deliverables.

[Meta's official WhatsApp pricing documentation](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing/) states that, from July 1, 2025, template messages are charged per delivered message according to category and recipient country; non-template messages are free within an open customer-service window, with additional category-specific rules. This volatility reinforces separate provider-cost disclosure and an approval date rather than a bundled IronWake price claim.

The [Wati pricing page](https://www.wati.io/pricing/) was fetched 2026-07-28 and advertises a trial and zero setup fees, but its dynamic content was not used to adopt a plan price. Search snippets and vendor-reported prices are not treated as primary evidence.
