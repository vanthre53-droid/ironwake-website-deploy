## Least privilege and recovery rules

- Record two human owners where practical, role-by-role admin access, MFA completion, recovery path, access-review cadence, and disconnect/removal procedure.
- LinkedIn Pages use individual-member access; there is no Page password. Limit super admin to the owner/recovery owner and grant content/analyst roles only when needed ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a8024204), [role reference](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a550647/)).
- YouTube channel permissions provide granular roles without sharing the Google Account password ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9481328?hl=en-AW)).
- No automated outreach, unsolicited DM, follow, connection, comment, or engagement. Scheduling/analytics/inbox tools require separate G1/GS1 approval, minimum permission, auditability, and disconnect path.

## Human action sequence per active platform

Current official eligibility/policy check → owner/recovery/admin/MFA record → live handle availability/URL check → profile/asset/copy preview → named approval → human login/edit/publish → logged-out desktop/mobile crop/link/contact verification → dated evidence and rollback/disconnect record.
