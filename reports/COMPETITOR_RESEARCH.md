## Pricing observations—not an IronWake price recommendation

- RingCentral's fetched page currently shows USD 49/month standalone with 100 minutes and a separate bundled route; this is product- and market-specific and must not be used as an IronWake service-price anchor.
- Smith.ai's fetched page asks for company, industry, and call-volume information; any plan comparison must be rechecked from its live commercial flow rather than inferred from a search snippet.
- SHIM AI's page contains vendor-reported availability, language, technology, and activity claims. They are not validated client outcomes, provider callbacks, or IronWake proof.
- Meta/BSP costs and platform availability vary by market, message type, volume, policy, account eligibility, and the chosen provider. They belong in a dated provider-cost disclosure, not a fixed public claim today.

## Buyer friction observed across the category

1. Buyers need to know what happens when an answer, booking, or provider call fails.
2. AI/voice claims are frequently grouped with broad outcome claims; IronWake can instead show status labels and owner confirmation boundaries.
3. Bundled monthly plans conceal variable telephony/message/provider costs; IronWake should separate them.
4. A generic "book a demo" leaves uncertainty. A short audit request with an explicit received/not-yet-confirmed state is a defensible first conversion.

## Search/content opportunity

Create original, buyer-task-led material only after proof and approval: missed-inquiry recovery checklist, booking confirmation versus request explanation, lead-source preservation guide, and demonstration/pilot/verified-live proof standard. Do not reuse competitor copy, visual composition, reviews, or performance assertions.
