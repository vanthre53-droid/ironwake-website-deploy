## What competitors do that IronWake doesn't

### Pricing transparency
- My AI Front Desk: Clear $20-99/mo tiers visible on every page
- Synthflow: $29-499/mo with feature comparison
- SkipCalls: Single $199/yr price — extreme simplicity
- **IronWake**: No prices visible anywhere

### Interactive proof
- Synthflow: Live voice agent demo
- ElevenLabs: Audio preview on hover
- Linear: Live product speed demonstration
- **IronWake**: Text-only case studies with SVG backgrounds

### Hero clarity
- My AI Front Desk: "AI Receptionist for Small Business" — instant understanding
- Smith.ai: "24/7 AI Receptionists & Live Human Agents" — clear hybrid model
- **IronWake**: "Turn every serious enquiry into a clear next action" — benefit-focused but vague on WHAT

### Motion quality
- Linear: Subtle speed demonstrations
- Framer: Scroll-driven motion
- Webflow: Complex but purposeful animations
- **IronWake**: Basic fade-up reveals, signal rail dots
