# IronWake Social Operations Runbook — Internal Draft

Status: `DRAFT — NOT CONNECTED OR PUBLISHED`
Scope: Instagram-first preparation; reusable for other approved surfaces after ownership verification.

## Operating promise

- Intake remains available 24/7 where the approved channel supports it.
- Automatic acknowledgement may be used only after the mailbox/form/provider is verified.
- Human follow-up target: within one Asia/Kolkata business day.
- This does not claim 24/7 human support.

## Content cadence

Start with a sustainable minimum:

- 2 educational posts per week.
- 1 proof/limitation post per week.
- 1 founder/process post every two weeks, only if Revanth approves the wording.
- Stories/reposts only from approved owned assets.
- SF01, SF02, and SF03 are proposed pinned targets after GS1 approval.

No scheduler is connected by this document.

## Reply boundaries

AI or templates may:

- acknowledge receipt;
- explain IronWake's general process;
- ask one qualification question at a time;
- direct a person to `ironwake.dev@gmail.com`;
- identify language preference and route the inquiry;
- label a request as awaiting human review.

Human approval is required before sending:

- prices, discounts, quotes, proposals, guarantees, ROI, or timelines;
- legal, tax, privacy, refund, contract, or payment statements;
- claims about client outcomes, testimonials, integrations, uptime, or provider status;
- replies to complaints, threats, sensitive personal data, or account-security issues;
- any outbound DM, follow, connection, comment, or engagement action.

## Default acknowledgement draft

> Thanks for contacting IronWake. We received your message. Revanth will review it and follow up within one business day.

This is a draft. It must not be sent until the monitored route and sending provider are verified.

## Consent and opt-out

- Do not add a person to marketing from a comment or unsolicited DM.
- Record only the minimum message metadata needed for routing and follow-up.
- For marketing, record affirmative consent, source, timestamp, and opt-out state.
- Honour a clear opt-out and stop promotional follow-up.
- Do not import private-message history into the CRM by default.

## Escalation

Escalate immediately to Revanth for:

- complaint or refund request;
- suspected fraud, account takeover, or impersonation;
- legal, medical, financial, or safety-sensitive content;
- requests for credentials, identity documents, or payment details;
- any request outside the approved offer and proof ledger.

Use neutral status labels: `received`, `awaiting human review`, `needs clarification`, `closed`, `opted out`.

## Moderation

- Hide/remove only spam, credential theft, malicious links, harassment, or personal data exposure according to platform rules.
- Do not delete legitimate criticism merely because it is negative.
- Preserve a minimal moderation record: platform, content ID, reason, human decision, timestamp, and rollback path.
- Never automate comments, follows, likes, DMs, or connection activity.

## Analytics

Track only approved aggregate operational data:

- platform and content ID;
- impressions/reach when available;
- inbound request count;
- CTA clicks or email referrals when measurable;
- consent state;
- next action and outcome.

Analytics are not client results, revenue proof, or conversion guarantees.

## Disconnect and rollback

Every connected tool must have:

1. named owner and recovery owner;
2. minimum required permission;
3. documented disconnect path;
4. export/delete decision for stored data;
5. last verified timestamp.

If a tool misbehaves, disable the connection first, preserve durable lead records, and notify the owner. Do not delete production data as a first response.

## Publication gate

This runbook remains internal until W03 ownership, W04 profile verification, GS1 content approval, monitored contact verification, tool approval, and human publication approval are complete.
