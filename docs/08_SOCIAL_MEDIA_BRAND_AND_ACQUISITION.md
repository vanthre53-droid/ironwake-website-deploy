# Social Media, Profile, Content, and Acquisition Workstream

## Status boundary

The Personal and Agency Branding System and Platform/Profile Execution Pack already exist as plans. No platform is considered configured, verified, published, or connected until its live public URL and contact path pass the acceptance tests below.

This is not a footer-link task. It is the public trust and acquisition layer connecting IronWake's portfolio proof, founder reasoning, content, conversations, website conversions, and CRM attribution.

## Required outcomes

1. Keep one coherent founder-led agency architecture: the founder explains decisions; IronWake owns formal offers, proof, standards, and commercial routes.
2. Configure only relevant platforms, one at a time, using real owners, real recovery controls, and current eligibility rules.
3. Convert verified portfolio proof into platform-native content without inventing clients, results, metrics, integrations, testimonials, followers, badges, or partnerships.
4. Route every CTA to a real monitored website, email, form, or approved conversation channel.
5. Capture social source, campaign, content asset, first touch, last touch, consent, conversation, next action, and outcome in the owner CRM.
6. Establish a sustainable publish-measure-improve loop instead of opening many empty accounts.

Do not create an ineligible marketplace or business profile, misstate age/ownership, or bypass identity, guardian, KYC, payment, or platform rules. Human owners perform login, CAPTCHA, MFA, identity checks, acceptance of terms, KYC, billing, and final publication.

## Per-platform execution contract

For one platform at a time:

1. Verify current platform eligibility, ownership, account type, API/automation, messaging, and content rules from official sources.
2. Record human owner, recovery owner, admins, least-privilege access, disconnect procedure, and current public URL.
3. Check handle availability live; never claim an unconfirmed handle.
4. Prepare every profile field, avatar/cover specification, CTA, contact route, link, pinned/featured content, highlights/playlists, and proof labels.
5. Validate every public claim against the approved claim and portfolio evidence ledgers.
6. Prepare a preview packet and stop for human approval before creating, editing, publishing, sending, linking, or connecting externally.
7. After the human action, verify the public page logged out, visual crop at real sizes, every link, contact conversion, permissions, and disclosure/proof labels.
8. Store a dated completion record and screenshots/evidence. Do not mark complete from a successful save screen alone.

Never request or store passwords, MFA/recovery codes, identity documents, card data, or raw long-lived tokens.

## Repurposing and cadence

Create one evidence-backed source asset and adapt it by platform:

- LinkedIn founder: first-person reasoning, decision, lesson, or concise case study.
- IronWake LinkedIn: formal framework, proof, offer, or standards update.
- Instagram: visual carousel plus a short demonstration when the asset supports it.
- Reel/Short: concise workflow demonstration with captions and truthful limitations.
- GitHub: technical evidence, README, tests, architecture, and limitations.
- Website: durable proof page/case study.

Suggested initial sustainable cadence after the foundation is live: founder LinkedIn up to two substantive posts weekly, IronWake LinkedIn one proof/process post weekly, Instagram one carousel plus one short demonstration weekly. This is a starting operating hypothesis, not a guarantee; adjust from capacity and measured qualified conversations.

Do not duplicate identical posts across all accounts at the same time. Do not automate unsolicited DMs, connection activity, comments, follows, or engagement. Official APIs or approved tools may assist drafting, scheduling, analytics, or inbox handling only within current platform rules, minimum permissions, human approval, auditability, and a disconnect path.

## Social-to-CRM data contract

The CRM must support:

- platform and account;
- content asset/campaign ID;
- source and medium;
- first-touch and last-touch timestamps;
- landing page and CTA;
- public conversation reference without copying unnecessary private message content;
- consent/opt-out state;
- assigned owner, stage, next action, and due date;
- meeting/inquiry/proposal/revenue outcome;
- content cost/time and approved performance metrics.

Use tagged links where appropriate, server-side lead-source preservation, deduplication, and privacy-minimised records. Never expose private DMs or CRM data publicly. Analytics do not replace CRM evidence.

## Measurement and improvement

Measure useful business signals, not vanity alone:

- profile completeness and logged-out availability;
- CTA/link/contact-route success;
- qualified replies and inquiries;
- meetings requested and confirmed;
- lead-to-next-action time;
- source-to-proposal and source-to-sale when real data exists;
- content production time;
- repeated buyer questions, objections, and proof gaps.

Followers, reach, likes, and views may be recorded, but they cannot be presented as business impact without a verified downstream relationship.

## Completion gate GS1

Social foundation is complete only when:

- the approved priority profiles exist and pass logged-out verification, or are explicitly deferred without fake website links;
- owners, recovery, admins, and permissions are recorded;
- all public copy, visual assets, proof labels, and contact routes are approved and working;
- nine foundation assets and three pinned assets are approved/published for the active launch platform;
- every CTA reaches a monitored destination;
- website social links use only verified URLs;
- social lead-source fields and CRM attribution pass an end-to-end test;
- publishing, response, moderation, access review, and disconnection runbooks exist;
- no unsupported claim or unapproved automation is live.
