## Actual Stitch defects to correct

Archive-level audit found:

- 30 isolated HTML pages rather than one application;
- desktop export only;
- Tailwind CDN/config repeated per page;
- only two HTML `<form>` elements and ten pages containing form controls;
- 169 external URLs/hotlinks;
- all pages contain inline scripts;
- fabricated or unapproved prices, including ₹1,25,000, ₹3,75,000, and ₹7,50,000+ in one screen;
- unsupported metrics/claims including audit sample sizes, conversion changes, arrival rates, and benchmark statements;
- Google-hosted generated imagery and generic photos without an ownership/license ledger;
- UI success states that may imply provider/database completion without proof;
- multiple screen-specific headers/footers/controls that must become shared components;
- design annotations and prototype-only copy that must not ship to public users.

Every one of these is a migration task, not a reason to regenerate Stitch.
