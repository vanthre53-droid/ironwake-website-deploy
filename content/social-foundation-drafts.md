## SF08 — Demonstration vs Pilot vs Verified Live

**Hook:** How should you interpret a case example?

A **demonstration** shows what a system can be designed to do.

A **pilot** is a limited, agreed trial with a defined owner and scope.

**Verified live** means the connected system has reproducible provider and database evidence.

IronWake keeps these labels separate so visual polish is not confused with operating proof.
