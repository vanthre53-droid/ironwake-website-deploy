Read `docs/08_SOCIAL_MEDIA_BRAND_AND_ACQUISITION.md`, the Personal and Agency Branding System, the Platform/Profile Execution Pack, current P0/P1/P1.5 reports, claim/asset/proof ledgers, real-data intake, approvals, and durable state before acting.

First audit the actual current profile state. Do not infer that a drafted bio, old screenshot, or account name means a live configured account. Create a dated platform matrix showing: URL, handle, account type, human owner, recovery owner, admins, eligibility, profile completeness, public proof, CTA/contact path, logged-out test, content foundation, permissions, and status.

Research current official platform rules and relevant competitor/benchmark patterns without copying them. Then prepare one consolidated social decision packet covering platform priority, founder-versus-IronWake roles, handle choices, approved profile fields, assets, nine foundation source assets, three pinned assets, cadence, response rules, CRM attribution, measurement, cost, risks, and exact missing data.

Drafting and internal asset preparation may proceed inside the approved phase. Stop for named human approval before any account creation/edit, publishing, message, external link change, API/tool connection, accepted terms, paid action, or permission change. The human performs login, CAPTCHA, MFA, identity/KYC/guardian steps, billing, and final publication.

Execute one platform at a time. After human action, verify logged out, test every real CTA/contact route, record permissions and recovery, store evidence, and update the website's social-link registry only with verified URLs. Create no fake profile or placeholder link.

Prepare the nine evidence-backed foundation assets and platform-native adaptations. Never invent clients, metrics, testimonials, integrations, followers, partnerships, awards, staff, or provider success. Every portfolio asset uses its approved demonstration/provider/live status.

Connect social attribution to the CRM using source, medium, campaign/content ID, first/last touch, consent, owner, next action, and outcome. Do not copy unnecessary private messages into the CRM. Test one authorised social-origin inquiry end to end before passing GS1.

Do not automate unsolicited outreach or engagement. Use only current permitted tools/APIs, minimum permissions, human approval, audit logs, rate/consent controls, and a disconnect path.

Return the platform matrix, asset/content register, approval packet, execution records, verified URL registry, CRM attribution evidence, remaining blockers, and GS1 result. Do not call this workstream complete until the acceptance gate passes.

GS1 is a precondition for P2/P3 public website implementation. Update every durable state file. If a platform is unavailable or intentionally deferred, record that decision and omit it from the website instead of blocking the approved minimum foundation.
